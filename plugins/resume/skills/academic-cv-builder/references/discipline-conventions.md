# Discipline conventions

Broad tendencies by area. Individual fields and departments vary, so treat
these as defaults to confirm with the user, not rules.

## Sciences (biology, chemistry, physics)

- Author order matters: first author leads the work, last author is the senior
  author.
- Impact factors are sometimes included.
- Numbered publication lists are common.
- Conference presentations carry less weight than publications.

## Humanities (history, literature, philosophy)

- Single-author publications are highly valued.
- Book publications are crucial.
- Conference presentations are important.
- Public scholarship is valued.

## Social sciences

- Both solo and collaborative work are valued.
- A mix of journal articles and books is expected.
- Funded research is important.
- Policy impact is valued.
