# Tailoring examples

Worked before-and-after examples for each adjustment. All people, numbers and
employers are illustrative; in real output every fact comes from the user.

## Professional summary

The summary is the pitch; rewrite it for each application.

Generic (avoid):

```text
Results-driven professional with 5 years of experience in business operations.
Strong analytical and communication skills. Looking for a challenging
opportunity to grow.
```

Tailored for an Operations Manager role:

```text
Operations Manager with 5 years optimizing supply chain processes and reducing
costs by 25%. Expertise in Lean Six Sigma, vendor management, and
cross-functional team leadership. Track record of improving operational
efficiency while maintaining quality standards.
```

The same person, tailored for a Project Manager role:

```text
Project Manager with 5 years leading cross-functional initiatives from concept
to delivery. PMP-certified with expertise in Agile methodology, stakeholder
management, and budget oversight. Track record of on-time, under-budget
project delivery across $10M+ portfolios.
```

Both versions draw on the same true history; each selects different parts.

## Experience: swap bullet order

Lead each role with the bullets most relevant to the target.

Applying for a management role, lead with:

- "Led team of 12 ..."
- "Managed budget of $2M ..."

Applying for a technical role, lead with:

- "Developed automated system ..."
- "Analyzed 500K+ data points ..."

## Experience: adjust bullet language

Work the posting's keywords in while staying truthful.

- The posting says: "stakeholder management"
- The bullet says: "Worked with various teams"
- Tailored: "Managed stakeholder relationships across 5 departments, ensuring
  alignment on project priorities"

The added detail (five departments, alignment on priorities) must come from
the user. Ask for it; don't supply it.
