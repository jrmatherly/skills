# Section formats

Layouts and model text for each section. Names, employers and figures are
illustrative; real output uses only the user's facts.

## Professional summary

One model per career stage.

Entry level:

```text
Recent Computer Science graduate from UC Berkeley with internship experience
in full-stack development. Skilled in Python, React, and AWS. Built
academic projects in machine learning and user-facing application
development.
```

Mid-career:

```text
Product Manager with 6 years driving B2B SaaS products from concept to scale.
Track record of launching products that generated $10M+ ARR through
data-driven roadmap prioritization and cross-functional leadership. Expert in
API products, developer tools, and enterprise sales motions.
```

Senior or executive:

```text
Technology executive with 15+ years building and scaling engineering
organizations from 50 to 500+ across global markets. Proven success leading
digital transformation initiatives, M&A integration, and platform
modernization. P&L ownership of $100M+ with track record of 40%+ efficiency
improvements.
```

Career changer:

```text
Sales professional transitioning to Customer Success, bringing 5 years of
consultative selling experience and proven ability to build lasting client
relationships. Skilled in needs assessment, solution design, and stakeholder
management. Brings relationship-building expertise to customer
retention and expansion.
```

## Experience

Standard entry:

```text
COMPANY NAME | City, State
Job Title | Start Date - End Date

- Achievement bullet with metric and impact
- Achievement bullet with metric and impact
- Achievement bullet with metric and impact
```

Multiple roles at the same company (company once, roles newest first):

```text
COMPANY NAME | City, State
Senior Manager | 2021 - Present
- [Bullets for current role]

Manager | 2019 - 2021
- [Bullets for previous role]

Analyst | 2017 - 2019
- [Bullets for earliest role]
```

Short tenure:

- Include it if the experience is relevant.
- Frame it around a project or an achievement.
- Don't apologize or explain on the resume.

Contract or freelance work:

```text
Freelance Product Consultant | 2022 - Present
Clients include: Company A, Company B, Company C
- [Achievement with Client A]
- [Achievement with Client B]
```
