# Reference list builder

Help the user choose the right references, prepare them, and hand an employer
a clean reference sheet at the right moment.

Before producing output, read
[templates/reference-plan.md](templates/reference-plan.md) and follow it. When
the user needs the sheet they will send to an employer, also read
[templates/reference-sheet.md](templates/reference-sheet.md) and follow it.

## Inputs to gather

- Target role and company, and the stage the application is at.
- Candidate references: name, current title and company, phone, professional
  email, how and when they worked with the user.
- For each one: whether they have agreed, how recently they were in touch, and
  how enthusiastic they are likely to be.
- What the user wants each reference to speak to (projects, skills).
- Complications: a confidential search, a manager who has left, a strained
  relationship, little work history.
- The user's resume header, so the sheet can match it.

## Truthfulness

Never invent a reference, a title, a company, contact details, a working
relationship or dates. Use only what the user supplies and leave a bracketed
placeholder for anything missing. Do not suggest overstating a relationship
(for example calling a peer a supervisor).

## Choosing references

Rank candidates by how well they can speak to the user's work:

1. **Most valuable:** a recent direct supervisor.
2. **Very valuable:** senior leaders who know the user's work.
3. **Valuable:** peers and cross-functional partners.
4. **Acceptable:** clients, vendors, professors or advisors (the natural
   choice for recent graduates).
5. **Avoid:** friends, family, and HR contacts who can only confirm dates.

Direct reports are worth adding for leadership roles.

Steer the user away from:

- The current employer, unless it has given permission.
- People who barely know the user or their work.
- A list made up only of references from 10+ years ago.
- Personal friends, unless the employer asks for a personal reference.
- Anyone likely to be lukewarm. A reference who won't be clearly positive is
  not used at all: fewer references beat a weak one.
- Anyone the user has not contacted in advance.

Aim for 3-5 references (more only if requested), at least one direct
supervisor, and a mix that covers different aspects of the user's work.
Identify one or two backups.

For a confidential search, a departed manager, a bad relationship with a past
manager or limited experience, see
[references/special-situations.md](references/special-situations.md).

## Process

1. **Select.** Rank the candidates as above, pick primaries and backups, and
   note what each can speak to.
2. **Ask permission.** Before anyone is listed, the user calls or emails to
   ask, confirms contact details, explains the role, and gauges enthusiasm.
   Draft the request from
   [references/outreach-messages.md](references/outreach-messages.md).
3. **Brief.** Each reference gets the resume, the job description, the points
   to highlight, the projects they may be asked about and the likely timing.
   Assign each key point to the reference best placed to make it. Warn them
   about the "would you rehire?" question; for it and the other questions
   checkers ask, see
   [references/check-questions.md](references/check-questions.md).
4. **Format the sheet.** A separate page from the resume, with the same
   header, fonts and styling, titled "References" or "Professional
   References", most senior or most relevant reference first.
5. **Follow up.** After the check, the user thanks every reference whatever
   the outcome, tells them the result and offers to reciprocate.

## Sheet contents

- **Required per reference:** full name, current job title, current company,
  phone, professional email, relationship to the user (how they worked
  together and when).
- **Optional:** LinkedIn URL, best time to reach, preferred contact method.

## Timing

| Stage | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Application | Do not attach references unless asked. "References available upon request" on the resume is optional. |
| First interview | Have the list ready; do not offer it. |
| Final rounds | Share the list when asked, usually after the final interview. |
| Reference check | The company contacts the references. |
| Offer | Follow up with the references and thank them. |

Confirm permission again each time the list goes to a new employer.

## Before finishing

- 3-5 references ready, with backups identified.
- Every reference has given permission and knows about this specific role.
- Contact information is current and accurate, as confirmed by the user.
- Each reference has the resume and their talking points.
- At least one direct supervisor is included.
- The set spans different aspects of the user's work.
- References are enthusiastic, not merely willing.
- Thank-you notes are planned.
