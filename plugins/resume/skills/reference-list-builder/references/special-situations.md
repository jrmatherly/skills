# Special situations

Options to offer when the standard "recent direct supervisor" is not
available. Present the options and let the user choose.

## Current employer doesn't know about the search

- Ask whether the reference check can wait until a later stage, such as after
  a conditional offer.
- Use colleagues who have left the company.
- Be upfront with the recruiter: "My current employer doesn't know I'm
  looking."
- Use other professional references from earlier roles.

## Manager left the company

- Track them down on LinkedIn.
- Use their personal email or phone, with their agreement.
- List them under their new company.
- Explain it in the relationship line: "Former manager, now at [New
  Company]".

## Bad relationship with a past manager

- Use another supervisor from that role.
- Use senior colleagues instead.
- Choose references from different roles.
- Prepare a short, neutral explanation in case the employer asks why that
  manager is not listed.

## Limited professional experience

- Professors or academic advisors.
- Internship supervisors.
- Volunteer organization leaders.
- Coaches or mentors.
- Client contacts.
