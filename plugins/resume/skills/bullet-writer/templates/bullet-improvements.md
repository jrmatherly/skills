# Bullet improvements template

Repeat the block for every bullet. The sample content shows the shape; replace
it with the user's bullets and facts.

```markdown
## Bullet improvements

### Original bullet #1
"Managed customer accounts"

### Issues
- Generic verb that hides what was done
- No metrics
- Vague (what did managing involve?)
- No result shown

### Improved version
"Grew portfolio of 40 enterprise accounts from $2M to $3.5M ARR (75% growth)
through quarterly business reviews and proactive upselling strategy"

### What changed
- Added scale (40 accounts)
- Added growth metric (75%, $2M to $3.5M)
- Specified actions (QBRs, upselling)
- Shows business impact (revenue growth)

### Needs from you
- [Any fact still missing or unconfirmed, shown as a placeholder above]

---

### Original bullet #2
[Continue for each bullet]
```

Omit "Needs from you" for a bullet with nothing open.
