## Worked rewrite

1. Start with the weak bullet: "Managed social media".
2. Ask: which platforms? How many followers at the start and end? What
   specific actions? What business impact?
3. Collect the answers: Instagram and LinkedIn; 5K followers at the start,
   18K at the end; daily content calendar and 15 influencer partnerships; 200
   leads generated.
4. Apply X-Y-Z: "Grew Instagram and LinkedIn following by 260% (5K to 18K) by
   implementing daily content calendar and 15 influencer partnerships,
   generating 200+ qualified leads".
5. Cut to length, keeping the strongest facts: "Grew social media audience by
   260% (5K to 18K followers) through content strategy and influencer
   partnerships, generating 200+ leads".
