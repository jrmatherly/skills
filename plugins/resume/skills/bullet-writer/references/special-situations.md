# Special situations

Example figures are illustrative.

## Entry-level and recent graduates

Draw bullets from:

- Academic projects with real impact
- Internship achievements
- Relevant coursework projects
- Volunteer work with quantifiable results
- Club leadership, with its numbers

Example: "Led university marketing club of 50 members, organizing 8 events
attended by 300+ students and securing $10K in corporate sponsorships".

## Career changers

- Lead with transferable skills.
- Reframe existing experience in the target industry's terms, without changing
  what happened.
- Highlight relevant projects and side work.
- Show learning and adaptation.

Example, from retail management toward a cross-functional role:

```
Before: Managed cross-functional teams of 15
After:  Led cross-functional teams of 15 across operations, merchandising,
        and customer experience, coordinating workflows and achieving 100%
        project completion rate
```

For the wider translation of a career history, use the
**career-changer-translator** skill.

## Gaps in employment

Write bullets for what the person did in the period:

- Freelance or consulting work
- Volunteer achievements
- Side projects
- Professional development

Do not draw attention to the gap, and do not disguise it: cover the period
with real, relevant achievements.
