# Example report

A filled-in report for an invented candidate and posting. Every name, number
and salary below is illustrative; it shows the expected depth, not facts to
reuse. The "Better" bullets assume the candidate confirmed those numbers.

```markdown
# Job analysis report

**Position:** Senior Product Manager
**Company:** TechCorp Inc.
**Location:** San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)
**Salary range:** $140K-$180K + equity

---

## Overall match score: 76%

**Recommendation:** Excellent fit - apply within 48 hours
**Application priority:** High
**Posted:** 2 days ago

---

## Requirements breakdown

**Top requirements, ranked:** product management experience, B2B SaaS, API
products, SQL/data analysis, cross-functional leadership

### Required skills - 8/10

- [x] 5+ years product management (You have: 6 years)
- [x] B2B SaaS experience (You have: 4 years)
- [x] Agile/Scrum (You have: 5 years)
- [x] Cross-functional leadership (You have: strong experience)
- [x] Data-driven decision making (You have: 3 years analytics)
- [x] API products (You have: 2 years)
- [x] Roadmap planning (You have: extensive)
- [x] User research (You have: 2 years)
- [ ] SQL/data analysis (You have: basic Excel only) - gap
- [ ] Mobile product experience (You don't have) - gap

### Preferred skills - 4/6

- [x] MBA or equivalent (You have: MBA from UC Berkeley)
- [x] Developer tools experience (You have: 2 years)
- [x] Payment systems (You have: 1 year)
- [x] International markets (You have: worked with EU teams)
- [ ] E-commerce background (You don't have)
- [ ] Machine learning products (You don't have)

### Soft skills - 5/5

- [x] Stakeholder management (strong mentions in your resume)
- [x] Communication (you present regularly)
- [x] Strategic thinking (MBA plus senior experience)
- [x] Influence without authority (you've done this)
- [x] Customer empathy (user research experience)

### Keywords

- Hard skills: SQL and data analysis (5x), API product management (4x),
  Agile/Scrum (2x), mobile (2x)
- Soft skills: stakeholder alignment (3x), cross-functional leadership (2x)
- Industry and domain: developer-focused products (3x), B2B SaaS (2x),
  metrics and KPIs (7x)

---

## Strengths to emphasize

1. **B2B SaaS PM experience**
   - 4 years in SaaS, exactly what they want
   - Lead with this in the resume summary
2. **API product background**
   - Your developer tools experience is highly relevant
   - This differentiates you from other candidates
3. **Data-driven approach**
   - Your analytics background addresses their need
   - Emphasize metrics and data in every bullet

---

## Gaps to address

**Critical gaps:** None

**Major gaps:**

- **SQL/data analysis** - mentioned 5x; they want a PM who can query data
  independently
  - Strategy: don't avoid this gap. Address it in the cover letter: "While my
    primary analytics work has been in Excel and BI tools, I'm actively
    learning SQL and can currently write basic queries." Emphasize your
    data-driven mindset and collaboration with the data team.

**Minor gaps:**

- Mobile product experience - mentioned 2x; listed as required but not a
  dealbreaker, since the role itself is an API product
  - Strategy: don't mention this gap. If asked in an interview, pivot to
    transferable product skills.

---

**Recommended order:**

### Priority 2: keyword integration

**Where to add:**

### Priority 3: bullets to strengthen

They mention "metrics" and "KPIs" 7 times in total.

Current: "Led product roadmap"

and reducing churn by 20%"

## Cover letter talking points

### Opening hook options

1. **Company knowledge:** "I noticed TechCorp recently launched your API
   marketplace - I've spent the last 2 years as PM for a developer tools
   platform, and I'm excited about the opportunity to bring that experience to
   your growing API ecosystem."
2. **Mutual connection:** "[Name] on your product team mentioned you're
   looking for a PM to lead the API product line - my 2 years in developer
   tools and B2B SaaS background would be a strong fit."
3. **Problem-solver:** "Your posting mentions challenges in stakeholder
   alignment across technical teams - I've navigated this exact challenge in
   my current role, aligning engineering, design and sales teams across 6
   concurrent product initiatives."

### Body: address the match

- "Your requirement for B2B SaaS experience: I have 4 years with ..."
- "Your focus on data-driven decisions: In my current role, I ..."
- "Your need for API product expertise: At [Company], I ..."

### Addressing the SQL gap (optional)

"While my data analysis has primarily been in Excel and Tableau, I'm expanding
my SQL skills and can currently write basic queries. More importantly, I've
built strong partnerships with data teams and consistently use data to inform
product decisions."

---

## Red flags analysis

### Potential concerns: Minor

**Flag 1:** "Fast-paced environment" - appears 2x
- May mean: startup or high-growth company; long hours, ambiguity, rapid
  changes

### Positive signals

- Salary range posted ($140K-$180K); the width suggests a flexible level or
  room to negotiate
- Detailed job description - the company knows what it wants; a
  well-organized role
- Specific tools named (JIRA, Amplitude) - operational maturity
- Hybrid flexibility mentioned - modern workplace practices

### Company research needed

- Glassdoor reviews (look for patterns in 1-2 star reviews)
- Recent funding and news (layoffs? growth?)
- LinkedIn: how long people stay (high turnover?)
- Levels.fyi: verify the salary range is accurate

---

LinkedIn; research the company more deeply
**Week 1:** Follow up if no response after 7 days

**Interview process (from the posting):**
1. Recruiter screen (30 min)
2. Hiring manager (1 hour)
3. Product case study (take-home)
4. Team interviews (3-4 hours)
5. Executive interview (1 hour)

## Decision factors

### Reasons to apply

1. Strong match (76%) - you meet most requirements
2. Role aligns with your career goals
3. Salary range is appropriate for your experience
4. Company stage fits your preferences
5. You have unique relevant experience (developer tools)

### Reasons to hesitate

1. The SQL gap is real - prepare to address it
2. "Fast-paced" may mean high pressure
3. Company culture still needs research

### Overall recommendation

**APPLY - this is a strong opportunity**

You meet 80% of the required skills and 67% of the preferred skills. Your
developer tools and B2B SaaS experience makes you a differentiated candidate.
The SQL gap is addressable with honesty and emphasis on your analytical
skills. Apply within 48 hours while the posting is fresh.
```
