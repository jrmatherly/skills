# Red flags

A flag is a reason to ask a question, not a verdict. Turn each one found into
an item under "Things to clarify before deciding".

## In the offer

- Vague bonus language ("up to 20%")
- Equity with no path to liquidity
- A high base but no equity, at a startup
- A vesting cliff longer than one year
- No vesting acceleration
- Non-compete restrictions (have a lawyer review any the user is unsure of)
- Verbal promises that are not in writing

## About the company

- High turnover (visible in employee tenures on LinkedIn)
- Recent layoffs or reorganizations
- A manager who seems checked out
- Recurring themes in negative employee reviews
- Funding concerns
- No clear path to profitability

Check these against current sources. Company health changes fast, so don't
rely on remembered facts.

## About the role

- Vague responsibilities
- A role that seems to change during the interviews
- A worrying reason the position is open
- No growth path discussed
- Unrealistic expectations
