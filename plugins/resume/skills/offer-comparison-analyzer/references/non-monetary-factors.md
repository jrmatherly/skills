# Non-monetary factors

Score each factor from 1 to 10 per offer, higher always better. Where a factor
has sub-scores, average them. Compensation, the sixth factor in the matrix, is
scored from the totals, not from this file.

## Career growth

- Which role offers more learning?
- Which company or brand helps the next job search more?
- Which has the better promotion track?
- Which offers more scope and responsibility?
- Which manager will develop the user more?

Scoring example (illustrative):

```
Company A: growth
- Learning opportunity:  8/10
- Brand/resume value:    7/10
- Promotion potential:   6/10
- Scope:                 8/10
Average: 7.25/10

Company B: growth
- Learning opportunity:  7/10
- Brand/resume value:    9/10
- Promotion potential:   8/10
- Scope:                 7/10
Average: 7.75/10
```

## Work-life balance

- Expected hours
- Remote or hybrid flexibility
- Whether people actually use their vacation
- On-call requirements
- Travel requirements

## Team and culture

- Manager quality (often the single biggest factor in day-to-day experience)
- Team health and dynamics
- Culture fit
- Diversity, equity and inclusion
- Company stability and growth
- Values alignment

## Risk

Score so that a higher number means lower risk, keeping "higher is better"
true for every factor.

Startup versus established company:

- Funding runway
- Market position
- Company trajectory
- Equity risk (it could be worth $0)

Questions:

- What happens if the company struggles?
- How stable is this role?
- What is the severance policy?

## Location

- Commute time and cost, or none if remote
- Whether the offer requires relocating, and what that costs the user and
  their household
- Cost of living where the job is (flag it; do not compute it)
- Whether the work arrangement (remote, hybrid, on-site) is written into the
  offer or only promised

## Gut-check questions

Put these to the user after the numbers are done.

**The gut check**

- Which offer excites me more?
- Which would I regret not taking?
- Which aligns with my 5-year goals?
- Which would I brag about to friends?

**The Monday morning test**

- Which job do I want to wake up for?
- Which team do I want to work with?
- Which problems do I want to solve?

**The learning test**

- Where will I grow more?
- Which skills will I develop?
- Which looks better on my resume in 3 years?

**The risk test**

- What's the downside of each?
- Which failure would I regret more?
- What's my backup plan for each?
