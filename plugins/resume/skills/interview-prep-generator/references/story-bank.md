# Story bank competencies

A complete story bank has at least one story for each group below, and
ideally covers most of the situations listed. One story can cover several.

**Leadership**

- Led a team through a challenge
- Managed conflict
- Made a difficult decision
- Delegated effectively
- Developed or mentored someone

**Problem-solving**

- Solved a complex technical problem
- Fixed a broken process
- Handled an unexpected obstacle
- Made a decision with incomplete information
- Improved something proactively

**Collaboration**

- Worked with a difficult colleague
- Aligned cross-functional stakeholders
- Built consensus
- Partnered with other teams
- Influenced without authority

**Achievement**

- Exceeded goals or expectations
- Delivered under pressure
- Went above and beyond
- Took initiative
- Accomplished something they are proud of

**Failure and growth**

- Made a mistake and learned from it
- Received critical feedback
- Failed and recovered
- Changed approach based on what they learned

When the resume has no material for a group, tell the user which group is
empty and ask for an experience that fits. Don't stretch an unrelated story
to cover it.
