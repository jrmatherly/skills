# Interview prep output

Produce the pack in this shape. Repeat the story block for every story. Omit a
section only when there is nothing truthful to put in it, and say so.

```markdown
# Interview prep: [Position] at [Company]

## Role analysis

**Key competencies they'll test:**

1. [Competency] - Evidence: [from the job description]
2. [Competency] - Evidence: [from the job description]
3. [Competency] - Evidence: [from the job description]

## Predicted questions

### High probability (prepare thoroughly)

1. [Question] -> Use story: [Story name]
2. [Question] -> Use story: [Story name]
3. [Question] -> Use story: [Story name]

### Medium probability

1. [Question]
2. [Question]

## Your STAR story bank

### Story 1: [Name, e.g. "Product launch success"]

**Use for:** [Competencies, e.g. leadership, achievement, cross-functional]

**STAR:**

- S: [Situation]
- T: [Task]
- A: [Action]
- R: [Result with metrics]

**Short version:** [60-second version]

**One-liner:** [15-second version]

### Story 2: [Name]

[Same structure]

## "Tell me about yourself" script

[Two-minute pitch tailored to this role]

## Difficult questions

**[Question]:** [Prepared answer]

## Questions to ask

**For the hiring manager:**

1. [Question]

**For the team:**

1. [Question]
2. [Question]

## Company research notes

## Before the interview
```
