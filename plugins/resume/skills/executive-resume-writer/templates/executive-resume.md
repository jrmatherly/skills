# Executive resume output

Follow this shape. Replace every bracketed placeholder. Omit "Board and
advisory" or "Industry recognition" when the user has none. The positioning
notes come after the resume and are for the user, not part of the document.
A figure that is an estimate or awaits the user's confirmation appears in the
resume as a bracketed placeholder such as `[$N]`, never as a number, and is
listed with its basis under "Needs confirmation".

```markdown
# Executive resume

## Executive profile
[Headline, brand statement, track record, what they are known for]

## Core competencies
[Competency grid of leadership themes]

## Career highlights
[4-6 top achievements with metrics]

## Professional experience

### [Most recent role]
[Title | Company, location, dates | Company context line]
[Scope paragraph: mandate, reporting structure, P&L or budget]
[Labelled initiative bullets with outcomes]

### [Previous role]
[Same shape]

## Board and advisory
[Board, advisory and observer positions]

## Education and development
[Degrees and executive education]

## Industry recognition
[Optional]

---

## Positioning notes
- Key narrative theme: [The story the resume tells]
- Leadership brand: [What they are known for]
- Differentiator: [What sets them apart]

## Needs confirmation
- [Estimated, assumed or possibly confidential figure] -> [Source or question]
```
