# Translation examples

Five common moves, each with a reframed bullet and a term table. The figures
in the translated bullets are illustrative: they stand for details the user
must supply (a real proficiency rate, equipment value, revenue figure). Never
copy them.

The term tables pair a source-field word with the nearest target-field word.
They are prompts, not synonyms: use a target term only where it accurately
describes what the user did, and keep the original term (or name the real
tool) where it does not.

## Teacher to corporate trainer / L&D

Original: "Taught 25 students in 5th grade classroom"

Translated: "Designed and delivered curriculum for 25 learners, achieving 95%
proficiency on standardized assessments through differentiated instruction
and data-driven intervention strategies"

| Teaching term | Corporate term |
| --- | --- |
| Lesson plans | Training curriculum |
| Students | Learners |
| Classroom management | Group facilitation |
| Parent conferences | Stakeholder communication |
| Assessments | Learning evaluations |
| IEPs | Individualized learning plans |

## Military to corporate

Original: "Commanded platoon of 30 soldiers in combat operations"

Translated: "Led cross-functional team of 30 through high-stakes operations
in ambiguous environments, managing $2M in equipment and achieving 100%
mission completion rate"

| Military term | Corporate term |
| --- | --- |
| Platoon/Unit | Team/Department |
| Mission | Project/Initiative |
| Operations | Programs |
| Intel | Data/Analytics |
| Briefing | Presentation |
| Command | Leadership |
| Deployment | Implementation |

## Retail to sales / account management

Original: "Sold products to customers and met sales targets"

Translated: "Consistently exceeded sales targets by 125%, generating $500K
annual revenue through consultative selling and relationship building with
50+ repeat customers"

| Retail term | Corporate term |
| --- | --- |
| Customers | Clients/Accounts |
| Store sales | Revenue generation |
| Customer service | Account management |
| Upselling | Account expansion |
| Returns | Issue resolution |
| Visual merchandising | Brand presentation |

## Hospitality to customer success

Original: "Managed front desk and handled guest complaints"

Translated: "Served as primary customer contact for 100+ daily guests,
resolving escalated issues with 95% satisfaction rate and implementing
feedback processes that improved NPS by 15 points"

| Hospitality term | Corporate term |
| --- | --- |
| Guests | Customers/Clients |
| Reservations | Booking and scheduling management |
| Guest satisfaction | Customer success |
| Complaints | Escalations |
| Concierge services | Customer support |
| Event planning | Project management |

## Healthcare to tech / pharma

Original: "Provided patient care and maintained medical records"

Translated: "Delivered patient-centered care to 20+ daily cases, maintaining
100% compliance with HIPAA protocols and utilizing EMR systems (Epic) for
accurate documentation and care coordination"

| Healthcare term | Corporate term |
| --- | --- |
| Patient care | Direct client care |
| EMR/EHR | Records systems (name the product, e.g. Epic) |
| Care coordination | Project coordination |
| Clinical protocols | Standard operating procedures |
| Patient outcomes | Performance metrics |
| Rounds | Status meetings |
