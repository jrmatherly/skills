# Creative resume strategy output template

Produce the strategy in this shape. Fill every bracket with a concrete value
(font names, hex codes, measurements); leave a bracketed placeholder only for
facts the user has not supplied.

**Format:** .docx and .pdf
**Design level:** Clean and professional, minimal embellishment

### Version 2: Designed
**Use for:** Portfolio, networking, direct outreach
**Format:** .pdf (print-quality)
**Design level:** Full creative treatment

- Body: [Font name, weight, size]
