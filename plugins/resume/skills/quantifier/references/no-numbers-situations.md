# "I have no numbers" situations

Each example shows the shape of the result. The figures are illustrative; the
real ones come from the user.

| The user says | Approach | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "I was just one person on a team" | Quantify the user's own contribution | Part of team that launched product | Contributed 40% of front-end code for product launch reaching 100K users |
| "I don't have access to business metrics" | Quantify activities and inputs | Supported sales team | Created 50+ sales presentations and managed pipeline of 200+ prospects in Salesforce |
| "My job didn't produce measurable outcomes" | Measure the work itself | Wrote documentation | Produced 75-page technical documentation reducing new hire onboarding time by 2 weeks |
| "Results were confidential" | Use percentages or ranges instead of absolute figures | Increased revenue | Grew revenue by 40%+ year-over-year |
| "I was entry-level with limited impact" | Quantify throughput, accuracy and learning | Entered data | Processed 200+ records daily with 99.5% accuracy rate, exceeding team average by 15% |

For confidential results, check that the percentage or range does not itself
disclose what the user is bound to keep private.
