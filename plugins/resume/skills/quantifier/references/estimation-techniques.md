# Estimation techniques

Use these when the user has no exact figure. Every estimate starts from
something the user stated, is shown to them with its reasoning, and goes into
the resume only after they confirm it. Example figures are illustrative.

## Conservative estimation

Estimate low to stay credible.

- The user thinks they saved 100 hours a month: write "75+ hours".
- The user thinks growth was 50%: write "~40%".
- The user thinks they served 500 customers: write "400+".

## Approximation

Mark a rounded figure with `~`: "Improved performance by ~40%".

## Range

Format "X-Y" or "X to Y".

- "Managed team of 8-12"
- "Generated $100K-$150K in revenue"
- "Saved 20-30 hours weekly"

## Minimum bound

Format "X+" or "at least X".

- "Served 100+ customers daily"
- "Managed at least 15 concurrent projects"
- "Generated $500K+ in annual revenue"

## Share of a known total

Calculate from a total the user knows.

- The company had 1,000 customers and the user handled 20%: "Managed 200
  customer accounts".
- The team had 10 people and the user supervised 4: "Supervised 40% of team".

## Time-based calculation

Work up from a frequency.

- 5 clients a week x 50 weeks: "Consulted with 250+ clients annually".
- 30 invoices a day x 250 days: "Processed 7,500+ invoices annually".

## Inputs when outputs are unknown

When the business result cannot be measured, count the work:

- "Conducted 30+ customer interviews"
- "Analyzed 500+ data points"
- "Created 20+ marketing campaigns"

## Related metrics

When the target metric is unavailable, use a neighbouring one. No conversion
figure? Use traffic, engagement or another step of the same funnel.
