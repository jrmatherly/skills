# Sentence patterns for quantified bullets

Example figures are illustrative.

## Before and after

```
Improved [X] from [before] to [after], resulting in [Y]% improvement
```

"Improved page load time from 8 seconds to 2 seconds, resulting in 75%
reduction and 20% increase in user engagement"

## Scale

```
[Verb] [number] [things], resulting in [impact]
```

"Managed 25 concurrent projects worth $3M, delivering 95% on-time with zero
budget overruns"

## Volume and impact

```
Processed [number] [items] per [time period], achieving [quality metric]
```

"Resolved 50+ customer tickets daily, maintaining 98% satisfaction rating and
4-hour average response time"

## Comparison

```
Ranked #[X] out of [Y] in [metric], [context]
```

"Ranked #2 out of 45 sales representatives nationally, generating $3.2M in
annual revenue"
