# Industry and situation adjustments

## By industry

| Industry | Adjust the letter to |
| --- | --- |
| Tech / engineering | Mention specific technologies; reference GitHub, a portfolio or technical projects; show an understanding of their stack |
| Marketing / creative | Show some creativity in the letter itself, within reason; reference their campaigns or brand voice; include relevant metrics (engagement, conversion) |
| Finance / consulting | Use a more formal tone; lead with credentials and certifications; emphasize analytical rigor and results |

## By company type

- **Startup**: more casual; show scrappiness and a growth mindset.
- **Enterprise**: more formal; emphasize process and experience at scale.

## Situations

### Hiring manager unknown

Use one of:

```
Dear Hiring Manager,
Dear [Department] Team,
Dear [Company Name] Recruiting Team,
```

Avoid "To Whom It May Concern": it is too impersonal.

### Referral

Lead with it. Use only a referral the user has confirmed.

```
[Name] on your [team] team suggested I reach out about the [Position] role.
Having [connection to referrer], I was excited to learn about [Company]'s work
in [area].
```

### Underqualified

Do not apologize. Emphasize instead:

- transferable skills
- evidence of learning quickly
- genuine enthusiasm for the role
- related experience that compensates

### Overqualified

Explain the motivation:

```
After 10 years leading large teams, I'm energized by the opportunity to return
to hands-on [function] work at a company where I can make direct impact on
[specific area].
```

### Career change

For deeper translation of experience between fields, run the
**career-changer-translator** skill first.

```
While my background is in [Previous Field], I've been actively building [New
Field] skills through [courses, projects, etc.]. My experience in
[transferable skill] translates directly to [new role] through [specific
connection].
```
