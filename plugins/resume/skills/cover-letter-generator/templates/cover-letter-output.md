# Cover letter output

Respond in this structure. Replace every bracketed placeholder; leave none in
the delivered letter. Omit a header line (address, title) when the information
is unknown rather than inventing it.

````markdown
# Cover letter for [position] at [company]

## Analysis summary

- Match score: [from the job-description-analyzer skill, or "not scored"]
- Key strengths to highlight: [list]
- Gaps to address: [list, or "None"]
- Company research notes: [facts referenced in the letter, with source]

## Cover letter

```
[Name]
[Email] | [Phone] | [LinkedIn URL]
[City, State]

[Date]

[Hiring manager name, if known]
[Title]
[Company name]
[Company address]

Dear [Full name / Hiring Manager],

[Opening hook: 1-2 sentences built on company knowledge, a mutual connection
or an achievement]

[Bridge: 1 sentence stating the role and the interest in it]

[Body 1: 3-4 sentences connecting the strongest relevant experience to their
primary requirement, with specific metrics and results]

[Body 2: 3-4 sentences adding further value, addressing any gap, and showing
company research and culture fit]

[Closing: 2-3 sentences expressing enthusiasm, suggesting next steps and
thanking them]

Sincerely,
[Name]
```

## Alternative openings

**Option 1 (company knowledge):** [alternative opening hook]

**Option 2 (achievement-led):** [alternative opening hook]

## Talking points for the interview

- [Point from the letter to expand on]
- [Point]
- [Point]
````

A number the user has not confirmed appears in the letter as a bracketed
placeholder such as `[N]%`, never as a figure, and is listed with its basis
under a final `## To confirm` heading.
